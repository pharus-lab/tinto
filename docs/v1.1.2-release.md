# Tinto v1.1.2 release record

Writing coach notes that stay while the writer edits, Mark as solved, and an estimate of what the AI service costs. Same format as [the v1.1.1 record](v1.1.1-release.md).

## Why

In v1.1.1 a coach note disappeared as soon as the writer edited its paragraph: notes were tied to the paragraph's node, and an edit replaces the node. The writer wants each note to stay until **Check again**, **Dismiss** or **Mark as solved**, the coach to take solved notes into account, and an idea of what Translate and the coach cost.

## Release decisions

- **Notes follow the text.** Each note holds document positions mapped through every transaction, so it follows edits around and inside its passage. A note whose passage changed is labeled **Edited**; one whose passage was deleted stays in the sidebar without an underline. Switching to Markdown, a review that returns after the writer edited, and a restart find each passage again by its text, nearest its old position.
- **What clears a note.** Check again replaces the scope's notes with the new review (the old ones stay visible until it arrives, and stay if it fails). Mark as solved and Dismiss remove one note. Dismiss results clears the local results only. Turning the coach off hides its notes; changing the document's language removes them.
- **Solved and dismissed notes go to the next review.** The request carries up to 40 of them for the scope, each with the passage as it reads now. The coach checks solved passages and raises one again with `repeat: true` (shown as **Still there**) if the problem remains; it does not suggest again what the writer dismissed.
- **Kept on this computer.** Notes per scope and the solved/dismissed list per document are stored in the app's local storage (`tinto-coach:<document>:…`), not in the `.tinto` project, so the project format is unchanged.
- **Usage is an estimate.** Every request's reported tokens (OpenAI and OpenRouter `usage`, Claude `message.usage`, priced by the model that answered, incomplete answers and refusals included) are priced from the published standard rates of 2026-11-23, or OpenRouter's reported `cost` when present. Models chosen with Other… count tokens only. The total since the writer started or reset is kept in `ai-usage.json` in the app's data folder: numbers only, no text or keys. Settings shows it as a bar split between Translate and the writing coach, with Reset.

The new and changed tests:

- `tests/writing-desktop.test.mjs`:
  - **Editing:** typing in another paragraph keeps every note, and revising the passage keeps its note, labeled Edited, with the underline on the new text.
  - **What does not clear notes:** Dismiss results keeps the coach notes.
  - **Solving and dismissing:** Mark as solved and Dismiss remove a note and announce the solved one.
  - **Persistence:** Markdown and back keeps the note on its passage, and a restart restores the notes and the solved list.
  - **The next review:** it receives the solved and dismissed notes with the current text, and shows Still there.
  - **Usage:** the AI service section shows the estimated spend, the split bar, unpriced tokens and Reset.
- `tests/writing-coach.test.mjs`: resolved notes are validated, limited and sent with every part; the instructions for solved and dismissed notes; `repeat` in the strict schema; the tokens each service reports, including OpenRouter's cost, an incomplete answer and a Claude refusal priced by the model that answered.
- `tests/ai-usage.test.mjs`: prices with cached input, OpenRouter names, unknown models, totals per task, concurrent records and reset.
- `tests/translation.test.mjs`: translation requests report their tokens.
